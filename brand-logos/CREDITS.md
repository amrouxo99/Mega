## card-logos/imtiaz.png
- Source: official EMTIYAZ (امتياز) app icon published by the **Ministry of Interior — Qatar** on the Apple App Store (app id 6473549296), retrieved 2026-08-12 via the public iTunes lookup API at 1024px.
- Processing: cropped to the wordmark, resized to 330×200, rounded corners. Colours and artwork unmodified.
- Use: shown in the "cards we accept" wall to state that MEGA accepts the Imtiaz discount card. MEGA is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or accredited by the Ministry of Interior; the card is accepted at payment only, per the approved wording on the page.
