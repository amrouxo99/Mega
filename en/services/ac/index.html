Air Conditioning

In a Qatari summer, 'the AC is blowing hot' is not a comfort complaint, it is a genuine emergency. A European car's AC combines the compressor, evaporator, condenser and precise electronic sensors, and one small fault — a refrigerant leak or a pressure sensor — can stop the whole system. We inspect item by item and repair only what needs it.

Our AC method is the opposite of the usual: instead of 'the system is gone, replace it', we start with pressure tests and dye-and-electronic leak detection, then an electronic read of sensors and valves. In documented cases that was the difference between a quotation in the thousands and a targeted repair that restored full cooling — as in our documented Jaguar XJ story.

We use factory-spec refrigerant and materials, and close every repair with a thermal performance test before handover, backed by a written warranty.

  • Precise leak detection instead of repeated refills that fix nothing
  • Repairing only the failed component, never a whole system unnecessarily
  • A documented cooling test before handover

Frequently Asked Questions

The AC cools then turns warm again. Why?

Usually low refrigerant from a slow leak, or a compressor cutting out on a pressure sensor. A refill alone hides the problem for weeks before it returns. Inspection finds the source.

How much is an AC service?

Individual written pricing after inspection. It depends on the fault source, and you see every item before approving.
